How the strategy works

The rule
  • Hold XLM. Your profit is counted as more XLM.
  • Swap all your XLM to XRP when XLM is at least 3% above its 10-day average price in XRP, the ratio has just turned back down (completed daily candle), and XRP has not crashed 20% or more in the last 7 days.
  • Swap back when your XRP buys at least 5% more XLM than you started with, after costs. Never swap back at a loss.
What history shows (Binance daily prices)

2020–2026: 36 swaps, about one every 2 months; 72% reached +5% within 30 days; about +48% more XLM per year. 2023–2026 only: 18 swaps, about +37% more XLM per year. Every swap eventually reached +5%; none stayed stuck. The worst swap was −55% on paper before it recovered, and the longest wait was about 7½ months.

Why XLM and not XRP as the base: XRP has slowly gained on XLM for years, and XRP's big jumps usually come from its own news and stick. XLM's jumps usually fade back or XRP catches up. Starting from XRP lost money in 2020–2022 and left swaps stuck for years.

These are past results, not a promise. The settings were chosen on the same history, so real results can be lower. Daily prices can miss moves within a day.

Settings

The signal rule itself uses the tested values (3% above the 10-day average, 7-day XRP crash limit −20%). Fee, slippage and targets change your swap-back price. Tested: +8% for the first 14 days, then +5%.
