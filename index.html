How To Use SwapAnalyzer
Simple rules. Follow them every time.
💡 WHAT IS THIS APP?
This app helps you swap between two cryptocurrencies at the best possible moment — when one coin is temporarily stronger than the other. You don't buy or sell for cash. You swap coin A → coin B, wait a few days, then swap coin B → coin A and end up with more of coin A than you started with.
THE 5 GOLDEN RULES
1️⃣
Only swap when score is 70 or above
The score is the single most important number. If it's below 70 — do nothing. Wait. Check again tomorrow. Green = act. Everything else = wait.
2️⃣
Never close a swap at a loss
If your position is currently red — hold it. The ratio will almost always recover. You only swap back when the score reaches 70+ again. Selling at a loss locks in the loss permanently. Patience recovers it.
3️⃣
Never hold the same coin in two trackers at once
If you swap XLM→DOT and ALGO→DOT at the same time, you are holding DOT twice. If DOT drops — everything drops together. Always spread into different coins across your trackers.
4️⃣
Check the app once per day — not more
Watching it every hour causes bad decisions. The score updates every 5 minutes but meaningful moves take days, not hours. Open it in the morning, check the score, act if score ≥ 70, close it. Done.
5️⃣
Reinvest at least 50% of every profit
Each time you close a successful swap, put at least half the profit back into your base stack. The bigger your stack, the bigger the next profit — even at the same percentage. Compounding is the strategy.
